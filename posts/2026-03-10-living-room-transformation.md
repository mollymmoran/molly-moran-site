After two years of staring at beige walls and builder-grade everything, I finally took the plunge. This is the full story of our living room makeover — from the initial panic-scroll through Pinterest at midnight to the moment it all clicked into place.

## Where We Started

The room had great bones: good light, nice proportions, and a fireplace that had real potential. But the gray carpet, the off-white walls with a vaguely purple undertone, and the furniture we'd accumulated over the years made it feel more like a waiting room than a home.

> "The goal wasn't perfection — it was a room that felt intentional."

## The Plan (and the Budget)

I gave myself a budget of $3,500 and promised myself I wouldn't blow it on one piece. Here's roughly how it broke down:

- Paint and supplies: $180
- New sofa (Article): $1,200
- Rug (Ruggable): $320
- Accent chair: $480
- Lighting: $260
- Decor, pillows, and art: $600

**Total: $3,040**

## The One Decision I Almost Didn't Make

I almost skipped painting the fireplace brick. It felt irreversible. But every mood board I made had a white or limewashed brick fireplace front and center. I finally did it with Romabio Classico Limewash in Mezzo White — and it transformed the entire room.

If you're on the fence: do it.
