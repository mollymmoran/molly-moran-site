I have tested more warm white paint colors than I care to admit. Sampled them on large boards. Lived with them across different times of day. Here are the ones that genuinely earned a place in my home.

## What Makes a Warm White Actually Work

The trap with warm whites is that they can read as yellow, peach, or just dirty depending on your light. The best ones have undertones that shift toward cream or greige rather than orange or pink.

## My Favorites

**Benjamin Moore White Dove OC-17** — the gold standard for good reason. Works in almost every light condition. Never yellow, always warm.

![](/images/img_0979.jpeg "Dining room update")

**Sherwin-Williams Shoji White SW 7042** — slightly deeper than White Dove. Perfect for rooms with lots of natural light where you want a little more depth.

**Farrow & Ball Pointing No.2003** — the most luxurious of the group. Rich without being dark. Worth the splurge if you're doing a small room.

## The One to Avoid

Benjamin Moore Navajo White. It reads orange in every light condition I've tried. Looks beautiful on the chip, terrible on the wall.
