About Me

Hi, I'm Molly.

Interior design enthusiast, first-time homeowner, and self-appointed creative director of our house-in-progress. My husband is equal parts handyman and hype man — and I couldn't do any of this without him.

We're based in Kentucky, living in a 1950s house with good bones, loads of potential, and a serious need for personality. I have no formal design training — just a lifelong love of all things home and creative.

True story: I bought this website domain three years ago (long before I had a house to design.) I reserved it hoping future me would work up the courage to do something with it. The name might sound fancy, but I promise this is just me — figuring it out room by room, project by project.

This blog is where I document the journey of turning our house into a home: the plans, the projects, and everything in between. I believe in designing slowly and intentionally, letting a space evolve around the people who actually live there.

Along the way, I'll be sharing what inspires me — designers I love, design principles worth holding onto, and the crafting projects keeping me busy.

I'm so glad you're here!

— Molly
