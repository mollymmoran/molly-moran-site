About Me

Hi, I'm Molly.

Welcome to Molly Moran Design — a space dedicated to the belief that a beautiful home isn't a luxury reserved for people with unlimited budgets or professional designers on speed dial.

I'm a home redesign enthusiast sharing everything I learn, spend, and discover as I transform our house room by room. You'll find honest before & afters, real cost breakdowns, my favorite sources, and the occasional mistake that taught me something valuable.

This blog is where I document all of it — the wins, the blunders, and everything in between.

Glad you're here.

— Molly
